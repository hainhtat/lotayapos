## Retry recovery review

Payment attempts now persist the serialized path/body in session storage under a per-user key before sending. Reload recovery reuses the same request, role-gates the recovery button, and blocks a new payment until resolution. The stored endpoint is restricted to the OS-payment create/replace routes; server authorization remains authoritative. Storage failure blocks sending instead of silently dropping retry protection. No concrete new money/security regression was confirmed in this flow.

Session storage is specific to the browser tab/origin. Recovery does not promise continuity after tab closure, switching hostname or switching browser. This is a residual scope limit, not a duplicate-posting defect demonstrated in the inspected flow.
